# Advanced Research risk and distribution

Supplied odds may be stale, include provider margin, or be entered incorrectly.
Manual Odds Research rejects missing provenance, future timestamps, and values
older than 30 minutes. Two-sided inputs expose de-vig and hold calculations,
but neither proves the user's estimate. Player and team events can be
correlated; the visible penalty is a sensitivity input, not a fitted model or a
guarantee.

The feature has no monetary-amount field, bankroll optimizer, operator or
affiliate link, automated action, or wager action. Current injury/inactive or
lineup-invalid signals reject affected legs. Missing or stale inputs remain a
watchlist, and negative expected-return math returns **No responsible parlay
found**. No supplied scenario history is stored by default.

Distribution is currently limited beta/sideload only. The unified extension
contains the manual-odds code even while it is gated off, so it must not be
submitted to the Chrome Web Store until current policy and legal reviewers
explicitly approve the package and its disclosures. A runtime gate is not a
substitute for distribution review.

Safeguards are layered: disabled/hidden by default, explicit informational-use
acknowledgement, a separate enable flag, direct-route enforcement, separate 21+
and jurisdiction acknowledgement, a 24-hour cooldown, local disable, and no
scenario persistence. These controls reduce exposure; they do not make
estimates accurate or eliminate user responsibility.

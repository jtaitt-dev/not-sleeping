# AI Dynasty, Rookie, Keeper, and Taxi

Local models keep market value, production value, contender value, rebuild
value, draft-pick scenarios, rookie eligibility, keeper context, and taxi
eligibility distinct. A prospect or veteran that violates the active league's
rules is excluded before an AI request.

AI may summarize timelines, strategic conflicts, uncertainty, and scenario
tradeoffs. It cannot turn unsupported age, contract, injury, or depth-chart
claims into facts. Player metadata changes invalidate related evidence, and
unknown Sleeper settings remain visible rather than guessed.

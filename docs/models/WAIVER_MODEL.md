# Waiver model

Waiver priority combines short-term, rest-of-season, dynasty/strategy, roster fit, scarcity, breakout/stash probability, and risk. Availability is calculated from live rosters and pending transactions. Drop score protects starters, holds, handcuffs, taxi value, and IR eligibility. FAAB bands use remaining budget, competing budgets when available, historical bids, urgency, and a rational cap; missing market history lowers confidence.

# IDP support

Not Sleeping preserves DL, DE, DT, EDGE, LB, ILB, OLB, DB, CB, S, FS, and SS rather than collapsing every defender into three groups. Broader Sleeper fantasy eligibility is retained for legal-slot matching.

Arbitrary non-zero IDP scoring keys are translated when raw fields exist; unsupported categories stay visible and lower confidence. Lineup, waiver, dynasty, auction, and draft services all accept IDP. The draft engine will not select an IDP merely because an IDP flag exists when the roster has no legal IDP slot.

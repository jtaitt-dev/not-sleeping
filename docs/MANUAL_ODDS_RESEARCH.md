# Manual Odds Research

Manual Odds Research is an optional Advanced Research workspace inside the one
Not Sleeping extension. It is research and education software, not wagering or
financial advice. The destination is hidden and its direct route is locked by
default. The user must first acknowledge the notice and enable Advanced
Research under **Settings → Analysis**, then separately affirm 21+ eligibility
and responsibility for local law inside the workspace.

Each usable leg requires a market, line, current American price, source, book or
consensus identifier, timestamp, estimated probability, and uncertainty. The
calculator converts prices, de-vigs a two-sided market when an opposite price is
available, reports hold, applies a visible conservative correlation penalty,
and shows probability, uncertainty, freshness, and return-index math.

The default player pool is the selected Sleeper team's legal starters for the
selected week. The user can explicitly include bench players, opponent
starters, and watchlist players. Injury/inactive state or removal from the legal
lineup invalidates an affected leg. Missing prices or values older than 30
minutes remain a **Prop Research Watchlist**. Only current, complete,
positive-value inputs can produce 2–4-leg educational candidates; otherwise the
result is exactly **No responsible parlay found**.

The workspace does not fetch or invent odds, discover markets, link to
operators, place an action, choose a monetary amount, optimize a bankroll,
promise an outcome, or persist supplied scenarios. A 24-hour cooldown cannot be
ended early from the workspace, and a local disable control is available. Only
the cooldown/disable preferences are stored.

`pnpm assert:unified-bundle` confirms that the feature is included in the one
build and that obsolete split-product labels and directories are absent. The
browser suite proves the global gate, direct-route gate, inner responsible-use
gate, and absence of stake and operator-link elements.

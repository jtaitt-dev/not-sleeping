# Trade Center

Trade Center accepts multiple assets and parties, separates market, production, dynasty, contender, and rebuild values, and reports starter change, depth, roster-space legality, fairness gap, and negotiation conditions. Superflex, TE premium, IDP, scoring, league size, strategy, and positional scarcity are inputs.

Current UI values derived from Sleeper search rank are explicitly labeled local proxies. They are not presented as market transactions. The extension analyzes a proposal but never sends an offer or changes a roster.

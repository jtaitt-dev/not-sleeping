# Parlay Lab risk and distribution

Supplied odds may be stale, include provider margin, or be entered incorrectly.
The workspace rejects missing source metadata, future timestamps, and prices
older than 30 minutes. Two-sided inputs are de-vigged and expose calculated
market hold, but neither calculation proves that a user estimate is accurate.
Real player and team events are often correlated. The correlation penalty is a
visible user-controlled sensitivity input, not a fitted correlation model or a
guarantee. Estimates can be wrong and do not predict outcomes.

Not Sleeping Labs is intended for GitHub sideloading by an informed adult. It
must not be submitted to the Chrome Web Store with Labs betting functionality
enabled. Users must affirm 21+ eligibility and are responsible for local law and
provider terms. A 24-hour cooldown, permanent disable, and a responsible-use
warning are present. The extension stores no scenario or sensitive betting
history by default; only the disable and cooldown preferences persist locally.

No monetary-amount field, bankroll optimizer, sportsbook link, affiliate link,
automatic action, or wager action is present. A current injury/inactive signal
or removal from the selected legal lineup invalidates the affected leg. Missing
or stale prices remain a research watchlist, and negative expected-return math
returns **No responsible parlay found**. Core contains none of the Labs route or
code.

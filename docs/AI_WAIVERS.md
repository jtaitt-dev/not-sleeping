# AI Waivers and FAAB

Waiver candidates exclude players already rostered in the current isolated
league snapshot. Local logic owns waiver type, FAAB budget, minimum bid,
drop-candidate legality, roster space, and Chopped roster-release behavior.

AI may explain role, schedule, uncertainty, and strategic fit. It never files a
claim, drops a player, or supplies invented news. Suggested amounts remain
bounded by the user's real budget and league rules, and every external fact must
be supported by visible evidence.

# Dynasty Center

Dynasty Center scores contender, balanced, productive-struggle, and rebuild paths independently and displays conflicting signals. The multi-year plan separates current production, market value, age/injury risk, depth, rookie/taxi assets, roster flexibility, and future-pick liquidity.

Future picks retain season, round, original owner, current owner, range, uncertainty, and years-away discount. Unknown future standings never become an invented exact slot. Orphan/dispersal contexts can be represented through manual strategy and ownership overrides.

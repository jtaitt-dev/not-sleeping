# Code of Conduct

Not Sleeping contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful,
and constructive.

Be considerate, welcome good-faith questions, critique ideas rather than
people, and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention,
or disclosure of another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may limit
participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be
reviewed with discretion and without retaliation.
